# Savings Quest — Lesson Content (for review)

This is the single, plain-English copy of every money lesson a kid can
see in Savings Quest, for a parent or content reviewer to read without
touching any code or JSON. **It mirrors `npc_lessons.json` exactly** —
if you edit a lesson here, update that file too (and vice versa); there
is no build step that keeps them in sync automatically.

Every lesson below follows the same rules: accurate, kind, never
judgmental, and never mentions investing, stocks, specific brands, or
any other financial product — just the plain concept named.

---

## Starter Meadow — Coach Penny (topic: saving)

> "Saving a little, often, beats saving a lot, rarely."

Every time you tuck money into Save, future-you says thank you! It
doesn't have to be a lot — small, steady amounts add up faster than
most people think.

## Whispering Forest — Sage Willow (topic: needs vs. wants)

> "A need keeps you going. A want makes things fun. Both matter — just
> know which is which."

Before you spend, ask: do I need this, or do I just want it right now?
There's nothing wrong with wants — just make sure your needs are
covered first.

## Coin Canyon — Canyon Keeper Cole (topic: budgeting)

> "A budget just means telling your money where to go, instead of
> wondering where it went."

You don't need a lot of money to make a plan for it. Even deciding
"this much for Save, this much for Spend, this much for Give" before
you get paid is a budget.

## Frost Peaks — Elder Frost (topic: emergency fund)

> "A rainy-day fund is money you save just in case — not to spend,
> just to feel steady."

Surprises happen: a bike tire pops, a gift gets forgotten. Having a
little extra saved up means a surprise doesn't have to turn into a
crisis.

## Sky Kingdom — Skyward Sage Luna (topic: patience and long-term goals)

> "Big goals are just small, patient steps that didn't give up."

The farther you can see your goal, the easier it is to keep going when
saving feels slow. Every bit you save now is a step your future self
already took.
